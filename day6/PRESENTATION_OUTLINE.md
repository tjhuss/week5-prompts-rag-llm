# Presentation Outline — AI News Intelligence Assistant

1. Title slide — AI News Intelligence Assistant, five-week build
2. The dataset — one news dataset, grown and reused every week
3. Week 1-2 — data pipeline and classical ML baseline (61-70% accuracy)
4. Week 3 — deep learning from scratch, why it lost to classical ML on this dataset
